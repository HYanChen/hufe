This application redistributes a derived city-point catalog containing GeoNames
identifiers, name aliases and WGS84 city coordinates. Dataset checksums and the
generation timestamp are recorded in city-centers.json. Public city coordinates
are used only to center a street map; they are not alumni locations or addresses.
